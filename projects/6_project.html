Roboticore Media Experience — ENSAM Rabat

Graphic design and media communication work for the Roboticore club at ENSAM Rabat, created using Adobe Photoshop.

🤖 Roboticore Club — Media & Design Experience

As part of my role in the Roboticore Club at ENSAM Rabat, I contributed as a media and communication designer, creating several digital posters and visuals to promote the club’s events, achievements, and workshops.

These projects allowed me to combine creativity, design precision, and communication strategy, using Adobe Photoshop as the main design tool.
Each visual was designed to engage our audience — both online and on campus — while maintaining the club’s innovative and tech-driven identity.

Selection of posters created for Roboticore events, workshops, and promotions — designed using Adobe Photoshop.

🧠 Context & Goals

The goal was to enhance Roboticore’s communication strategy through impactful visual storytelling.
Our team aimed to:

  • Promote events, challenges, and robotics workshops
  • Strengthen the club’s identity through consistent visual design
  • Share achievements and attract new members

These visuals were shared across social media, university platforms, and event exhibitions, helping to reinforce the club’s presence in the ENSAM community.

💬 Reflection

This media experience at ENSAM Rabat was an excellent opportunity to merge my passion for engineering and visual design, while contributing to a collaborative and creative club environment.

It also taught me the value of visual impact in communication, especially in promoting technological and educational initiatives.
