Retro-Modeling of a Tomato Press

A complete 3D reverse engineering study carried out using 3DEXPERIENCE Platform and CREO Parametric — combining design precision, PLM workflow, and collaboration.

🔧 Methodology

We applied the Skeleton Methodology to structure our assembly and guide the retro-modeling process, ensuring coherence, modularity, and precise parametric control throughout the CAD workflow.

🤝 Collaboration

Working closely with my teammate, we combined 3DEXPERIENCE’s PLM tools for collaborative project management and CREO Parametric for advanced 3D surface modeling.

🚀 Growth

This two-and-a-half-month project allowed me to master surface design, enhance my understanding of collaborative engineering, and even explore web development to build this digital portfolio.

📸 Visual Highlights

High-quality renderings of the tomato press — illustrating geometry reconstruction, detailed assembly, and refined surface modeling.

🎥 The Project in Motion

A short video documenting the design process and the final 3D model render.
