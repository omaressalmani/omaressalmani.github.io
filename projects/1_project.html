Retro-Modeling: The Tomato Press

A deep dive into surface modeling and parametric design, made possible by 3DEXPERIENCE and CREO Parametric.

⚙Methodology

The project utilized the rigorous Skeleton Methodology for robust design consistency and applied key PLM principles to manage our iterative process.

💻Technology Stack

A seamless collaborative workflow was established by combining the power of CREO Parametric for modeling with the data management efficiency of the 3DEXPERIENCE Platform.

💪Personal Growth

Over 2.5 months, I significantly enhanced my skills in complex Surface Modeling techniques and explored web development to create this interactive portfolio.

📸 Visual Overview

Rendered models of the tomato press — illustrating geometry reconstruction, assembly structure, and the complexity of the surface modeling challenge.

🎥 The Process in Motion

A short video showcasing the step-by-step modeling process and final renderings.
