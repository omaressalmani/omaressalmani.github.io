Retro-Modeling of a Tomato Press

Reverse engineering and 3D modeling of a tomato press using 3DEXPERIENCE and CREO.

I’m thrilled to share the outcome of an exciting engineering journey: the retro-modeling of a tomato press, a project that came to life through the powerful combination of 3DEXPERIENCE Platform and CREO Parametric.

Working closely with my teammate, we leveraged the advanced features of both tools while applying PLM methodologies to enhance our collaborative workflow. The Skeleton methodology served as our design compass, ensuring consistency and efficiency throughout the process.

Over two and a half months, I deepened my skills in surface modeling and refined my understanding of parametric design. Using CREO was an enriching experience, and the efficiency of collaboration through 3DEXPERIENCE exceeded all expectations.

To represent our project, I decided to build this portfolio — a decision that allowed me to explore web development, step out of my comfort zone, and blend design engineering with technology.

Huge thanks to my teammate for their dedication and to our professors for their invaluable guidance throughout this journey. This project has been an incredible adventure of continuous learning and collaborative growth! 🌟
