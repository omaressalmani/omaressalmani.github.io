🎨 UTC × Saint-Gobain — Event Poster Design

As part of an event organized by the Université de Technologie de Compiègne (UTC), I had the opportunity to design a poster visual for the Career Service to promote an exclusive immersion day at the Saint-Gobain Tower.

The goal was to capture the energy, innovation, and professional growth at the heart of this event — using bold typography, strong color contrasts, and modern composition to resonate with students and professionals alike.
The visual was created entirely using Adobe Photoshop, blending UTC’s identity with the dynamic spirit of Saint-Gobain.

Official event poster designed for UTC Career Service — created with Adobe Photoshop.

📢 About the Event

Hosted by UTC Career Service, the event invited students to spend a day at Saint-Gobain’s headquarters to explore sustainable innovation in construction and connect with industry professionals.

Guided by Karim Hechmi (“Tonton Karim”), this event offered a unique mix of inspiration, expertise, and networking opportunities for future engineers.

“An exclusive immersion day at Saint-Gobain — discover, learn, and connect with leaders shaping the future of sustainable construction.”
