### 🇲🇦 ArabeUT – Moroccan International Day Poster

As part of the **ArabeUT Association** at the **Université de Technologie de Compiègne (UTC)**,  
I had the opportunity to design the **official poster** for the *Moroccan International Day* event.  
This poster was created using **Adobe Photoshop** and shared across the **official ArabeUT and UTC social media platforms** to promote the celebration.

#### 🌍 Event Context

The **Moroccan International Day** is an annual cultural event organized by **ArabeUT** to celebrate Morocco’s heritage, diversity, and traditions.  
Through artistic showcases, culinary experiences, and cultural performances, the event highlights the richness of Moroccan culture within the UTC community.

My goal in designing this poster was to:
- Capture the **essence and warmth of Moroccan culture** 🌞  
- Use a **vibrant yet elegant composition** that aligns with the event’s festive identity  
- Ensure **visual harmony** with the ArabeUT branding and UTC’s communication standards  

---

#### 🧰 Tools Used
- **Adobe Photoshop** — layout design, color work, and typography  
- **Social Media Platforms** — ArabeUT official pages for publication and event promotion  

---

#### 💬 Reflection

This experience allowed me to blend **graphic design and cultural storytelling**,  
while contributing to an event that celebrates **diversity and unity** within the UTC community.  

Designing for an official association strengthened my sense of **visual responsibility** and **creative identity** as a student designer. ✨
